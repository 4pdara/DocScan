DocScan page network (pagenet-v1): training data attribution

DocScan's page network is a small neural network trained by the DocScan project. It finds the page outline on a
photo, on the phone. Its weights are DocScan's own; no other detector's models or outputs were used to train it.

It was trained on two sources:

1. Synthetic scenes drawn by DocScan's own generator (pages, covers and books on generated surfaces).

2. "A New Image Dataset for Document Corner Localization"
   Shima Dizaj, Mendeley Data, version 3 (2020).
   https://doi.org/10.17632/x3nm4cxr83.3
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   The dataset's authors ask that the following paper be cited when it is used:
   https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9116896/

Changes made to the dataset: the photos were reduced to 256 x 256 pixels, and their marked corners were drawn as a
page region and a page outline at that size. The photos were split by a hash of their names: 60% were used for
training (each one several times, with flips, turns and changes of brightness), and 40% were kept aside and used only
to measure the result. No photo from the dataset is included in the app.

The dataset's authors and Mendeley do not endorse DocScan or this use of the dataset.
